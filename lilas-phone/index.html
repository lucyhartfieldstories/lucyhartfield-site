Lila's phone

She usually avoids texting clients. She finds it too informal. Then the bride's brother got her number. Every text below is straight from the book (chapter ten).

‹NNate (Kat's brother)

After the $1,200 candle incident

Would Kat wanting to switch from buffet apps to passed hors d'oeuvres at the cocktail hour be considered major?
Calling you now.

The day before the hotel deadline

I just got the rooming report. Are you planning to stay at the hotel during wedding weekend? They don't have a reservation for you.
You just saved me. I gave Kat my notes sheet from the last meeting–it had my reminder on it.
No worries. The deadline is tomorrow though, so be sure to get it in.
I will. THANK YOU.
Henry mentioned you're one of his produce vendors. Will you have any batches over to him before the wedding?
I don't think I'll have enough to make it worth it for him.
why?
I wouldn't have pegged you for a gardener. I was just wondering if you're any good.
I don't have enough for Henry to make use of yet, but I am having a game night with Kat and Joel and some others this weekend. I was going to make a garden pasta. You're more than welcome to join. 7pm on Thursday.
📅Game night
Thursday, 7:00 PM
No pressure. Just a friendly game night. But heads up, Sarah is a little cutthroat.
Don't you think it might be weird? Kat and Joel are my clients. That might be crossing a professional line.
Kat said the other day she thought you'd fit in the friend group. If you want me to check with them, I can, but I think it would be fine.
Update. They said they'd "love for you to see the crazy before you see it at the wedding." So. Do with that what you will. You're welcome to bring a friend if that feels better.
What should I bring?
You, yourself, and absolutely zero work talk.
