Where to start with Tessa Bailey (from someone who's read twelve of her books)

September 2026. From Lucy's own shelf.

Tessa Bailey is the author I've read the most, full stop. Twelve books and counting. Her rom-coms are funny, big-hearted, and unafraid of big feelings, which is exactly the lane I'm writing in. If you're new to her, here's how her series break down and where I'd jump in.

Big Shots (sports rom-coms)

  • Fangirl Down by Tessa Bailey

    Book one. The series opener, and the one that made me keep going.

  • The Au Pair Affair by Tessa Bailey

    Book two.

  • Dream Girl Drama by Tessa BaileyLucy rated it 5 stars

    Book three, and a five-star read for me. If you only read one, make it this one.

  • Pitcher Perfect by Tessa BaileyLucy rated it 4 stars

    Book four.

Bellinger Sisters

  • It Happened One Summer by Tessa Bailey

    The one everyone starts with, and for good reason.

  • Hook, Line, and Sinker by Tessa Bailey

    The follow-up, with the other sister.

Romancing the Clarksons

  • Too Hot to Handle by Tessa Bailey

    Book one.

  • Too Wild to Tame by Tessa Bailey

    Book two.

Broke and Beautiful

  • Chase Me by Tessa Bailey

    Book one.

  • Need Me by Tessa Bailey

    Book two.

  • Make Me by Tessa Bailey

    Book three.

These are books I've actually read, with the star ratings from my own reading log. No sponsorships, no affiliate links, just my shelf. Ratings are on a five-star scale.

My pick for a first Tessa Bailey: It Happened One Summer if you want the classic, Dream Girl Drama if you want my favorite.
