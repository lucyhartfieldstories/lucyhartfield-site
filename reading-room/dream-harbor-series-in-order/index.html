The Dream Harbor series in order (and why I read all five)

September 2026. From Lucy's own shelf.

If you like your romance with a small town, a seasonal storefront, and a lot of baked goods, Laurie Gilmore's Dream Harbor series is the coziest shelf I own. Every book is a standalone love story set in the same little town, so you can start anywhere, but here's the order they were published, which is how I read them.

(Fair warning: this series is a big reason there's a bakery at the center of my own book.)

Reading order

  • The Pumpkin Spice Café by Laurie Gilmore

    Book one. A café, a new owner, and a town that's about to become your comfort place.

  • The Cinnamon Bun Book Store by Laurie GilmoreLucy rated it 4 stars

    Book two. A bookstore, a mystery of little notes, and the kind of romance you read in one sitting.

  • The Christmas Tree Farm by Laurie Gilmore

    Book three. Winter in Dream Harbor, exactly as cozy as it sounds.

  • The Strawberry Patch Pancake House by Laurie Gilmore

    Book four. Pancakes, a fresh start, and a lot of heart.

  • The Gingerbread Bakery by Laurie GilmoreLucy rated it 5 stars

    Book five, and my favorite of the series. A bakery, a holiday season, and a five-star read for me.

These are books I've actually read, with the star ratings from my own reading log. No sponsorships, no affiliate links, just my shelf. Ratings are on a five-star scale.

If you want the vibe of Dream Harbor with a wedding planner and one very late guest, that's the book I'm writing now. Newsletter readers get the sneak peeks first.
