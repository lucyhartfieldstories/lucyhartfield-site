Books on my shelf that handle mental health with care

September 2026. From Lucy's own shelf.

I'm writing a rom-com heroine with OCD, and I want to get it right: real, specific, and never the punchline. These are the books on my shelf that handle mental health, neurodivergence, and grief with the kind of care I'm aiming for. Not all of them are romances, and one isn't fiction at all.

Romance

  • The Kiss Quotient
    The Kiss Quotient by Helen Hoang

    Stella is a brilliant econometrician with autism who decides she needs practice at relationships, so she hires an escort, Michael, to teach her. Written by an author who is autistic herself, it's funny, sexy, and deeply respectful of how Stella experiences the world. The gold standard for representation in romance, for me.

Fiction

  • The Midnight Library
    The Midnight Library by Matt Haig

    Nora, at her lowest, finds a library between life and death where every book is a life she could have lived. A novel about regret and depression that lands, gently and honestly, on hope.

  • Remarkably Bright Creatures
    Remarkably Bright Creatures by Shelby Van Pelt

    Tova, a widow cleaning an aquarium at night, befriends a giant Pacific octopus who knows more about her missing son than anyone. Grief, loneliness, and unexpected connection. Gentler than it sounds, and more moving.

  • Out of Love
    Out of Love by Hazel Hayes

    A relationship told backward, from the breakup to the first meeting. Honest about how love and mental health tangle together, and about the parts of a relationship we only understand afterward.

Memoir

  • Group
    Group by Christie Tate

    A successful lawyer joins group therapy and slowly, messily, learns how to be known. Told with humor and total honesty. The subtitle says it all: how one therapist and a circle of strangers saved my life.

These are books I've actually read, with the star ratings from my own reading log. No sponsorships, no affiliate links, just my shelf. Ratings are on a five-star scale.

My own book puts a wedding planner with OCD at the center of a rom-com, with her checking rituals on the page and her happy ending fully earned. If you read for representation, the newsletter is where I'll share how it's going.
