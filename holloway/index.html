Welcome to

Holloway

Pop. a lot of cake and one very busy wedding planner

A small Midwest town with one great cafe, a whole lot of corn (or is it soybeans?), and a wedding coming up. Here's your visitor's guide.

A fictional town from Lucy Hartfield's debut rom-com

Around town

  • ☕

    Holloway Hearth Cafe

    Bakery, cafe & catering

    Henry's place. The best carrot cake in the state, a pastry case worth the trip, and Lila's corner table. See the menu

  • 📋

    Dream Details Wedding Co.

    Wedding planning

    Lila's business. Your dream day, down to the last detail. Visit Dream Details

  • 🏢

    Lila's walk-up

    Fourth floor

    Plants on the windowsill, paintings from friends on the walls, and an elevator that's out of order more often than not.

  • 🌶

    Nate's house

    Garden out back

    A deck, a garden fenced in chicken wire, and window boxes for the pollinators. The peppers and bush beans always come through. The cucumbers never do.

  • 🎲

    Game night

    At Nate's

    Monopoly gets cutthroat (that's Kat), the deviled eggs disappear (that's Trish), and the veggie pasta haunts your dreams.

  • 🌽

    The fields

    Just outside town

    Corn or soybeans? It's a whole debate. See chapter four.

Worth the drive

The wedding is two hours from Holloway, on the bluffs above a river valley. Pack a bag.

  • 🦅

    The venue on the bluffs

    Ceremony & reception

    Stained glass, a grand hall, and a lawn over the river valley. According to Cara, the bald eagles are the real VIPs. No candles. No animals. (Mostly.)

  • 🛏️

    The wedding hotel

    Room block

    River valley views. Sells out on peak weekends. If you're sharing a room, ask for two queens. Just trust us.

  • 🍝

    The Italian place

    Dinner nearby

    Checkered tablecloths, fake grapes hanging from the ceiling, and an obscene number of ceramic chef figurines. Voted best restaurant in the area five years in a row, according to its own sign.
