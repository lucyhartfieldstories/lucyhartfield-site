You're a Lila

You plan everything except falling in love. You show up 25 minutes early with a binder (and a backup binder), you believe in the florals and in fairytale endings, and you write to-do lists with things you already finished, just so you can cross them off. When something goes wrong, you feel it like the end of the world, then put on your best professional smile and fix it anyway. The right person will notice what's behind the smile, and stay.

"There are signs along the way, if you prove able to read them."

You're a Nate

You're The Fixer. Everyone brings you their decisions, and you make the practical call every time. You know the off-peak discount to the percentage, you'd bring a tape measure to a venue tour, and you ask questions nobody else has ever asked. But you're also the one who gets on the floor to help, covers for someone without being asked, and quietly clears your calendar to be there. You have layers.

"I'm not totally illiterate, just oblivious."

You're a Henry

You're the best friend everyone needs. You keep your community fed, you keep calm when everyone else is spiraling, and you've followed the safety handbook the whole time (you may have helped write it). You roll your eyes at chaos with a soft smile, and you always, always have a second batch in the oven.

"Only staff may enter the kitchen, for food safety and insurance purposes."
