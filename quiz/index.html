You're a Lila

You plan everything except falling in love. You believe in fairytale weddings with your whole heart, you carry an emergency box for other people's crises, and you write to-do lists with things you already finished. You feel every mistake like the end of the world, and you fix it anyway. The right person will notice the parts you work hardest to hide, and stay.

"There are signs along the way, if you prove able to read them."

You're a Nate

You're The Fixer. Everyone brings you their decisions, and you make the practical call every time. You think marriage is a business agreement (or you say you do), you know the off-peak discount to the percentage, and you'd rather clear your calendar than explain how you feel. Underneath the logic, you're the person who gets on the floor to help. You have layers.

"I'm not totally illiterate, just oblivious."

You're a Henry

You're the best friend everyone needs. You keep the community fed, you keep your head when everyone else is spiraling, and you've quietly followed the safety handbook the whole time. Award-winning, wholeheartedly trustworthy, and never, ever late with the cake.

"Only staff may enter the kitchen, for food safety and insurance purposes."
