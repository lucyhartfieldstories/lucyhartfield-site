Books by Lucy Hartfield

Warm, witty romantic comedies about imperfect people, the communities that hold them up, and love that shows up when it's least convenient.

Debut novel, title coming soon

An instant chemistry, opposites attract rom-com set in the world of weddings

Lila runs Down to the Details Wedding Co., and she lives by its motto: your dream day, down to the last detail. She writes the safety manuals, color-codes the timelines, and has her best friend Henry, the award-winning baker behind Crumb Together Cafe, on speed dial. What she doesn't plan for is the stranger who walks straight into her tray of tasting cakes.

His name is Nate. He's late, he's glued to his phone, and he's the bride's big brother, here to keep the wedding on budget and on track, which is supposed to be Lila's job. He thinks marriage is a business agreement. She believes in love with her whole heart. And yet he's the one who stays on the floor with her while she cries into the buttercream, and then quietly lies to the whole table to cover for her.

Somewhere between a rehearsal dinner and a hotel with only one bed left, the man who ruined her tasting starts to see the parts of her she works hardest to hide. One of them is going to have to be wrong about love. Neither of them expects it to be this messy.
