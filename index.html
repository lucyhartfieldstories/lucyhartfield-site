She plans every detail of other people's happily ever afters.

Lila is the wedding planner who sees everything coming, except the stranger who walks straight into her tray of tasting cakes. His name is Nate. He's late, he's glued to his phone, and he's the bride's big brother, which means he'll be at every fitting, every dinner, and every last-minute crisis until the big day.

He thinks marriage is a business agreement. She believes in love with her whole heart. One of them is going to have to be wrong.

About Lucy

Lucy Hartfield writes romance for readers who love sharp banter, soft hearts, and a happily ever after that feels earned. Her stories are about imperfect people finding love, and the communities that hold them up along the way.

She believes books are meant to be shared: passed between friends, argued about in group chats, and read far too late into the night. Her debut, an opposites-attract romantic comedy set in the world of weddings, is on its way.
