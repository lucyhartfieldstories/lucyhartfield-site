Help Lila with the seating chart

The Thompson wedding is coming together, and Lila's binder is open to a blank seating layout. Seat every guest so the whole family is happy. (Every rule here comes straight from the book.)

Lila's notes

  1. The couple: Kat and Joel sit at the head table. Obviously.
  2. The wedding party: Sarah (maid of honor) and Brian (best man) sit with them.
  3. Gramma Sue needs 36 inches of clearance for her wheelchair. Table one has the wide aisle.
  4. Uncle Joe goes at table six or table seven. (Lila and Nate debated this for an ungodly amount of time.)
  5. Tank is staying on the lawn the whole time, and he'll need a handler. Cousin Laura loves hanging out with Tank.
  6. Cousin Lena has celiac disease. Tap her place card twice to flag her gluten-free cake for Henry.

How to play: tap a guest, then tap a table. Or drag and drop on a computer.
