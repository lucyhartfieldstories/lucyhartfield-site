Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026. In plain language: your information is used only to send you the newsletter you asked for.

What we collect

When you join the newsletter, we collect your first name (optional) and email address. If you email hello@lucyhartfield.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to share.

How it's used

Your email is used only to send Letters from Hartfield: book news, sneak peeks, writing updates, and advance copy invitations. We never sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

Services we use

The newsletter is managed through MailerLite, which stores subscriber information and may use cookies or similar technology to deliver emails and measure opens and clicks. This website is hosted by Netlify and loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and cdnjs, which may receive basic technical information like your IP address when pages load.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of every email. To have your information deleted or to ask a question about your data, write to hello@lucyhartfield.com.

Children

This website and newsletter are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13.
