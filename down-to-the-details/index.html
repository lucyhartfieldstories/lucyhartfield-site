Down to the Details

Wedding Co. · Holloway

Your dream day, down to the last detail.

A fictional business from Lucy Hartfield's debut rom-com

Hi, I'm Lila

I plan weddings for couples who want their wildest-dreams day and a plan that actually works. I build the vision boards, write the timelines, color-code the binder (and the backup binder), and handle every detail you'd rather not think about, so all you have to do is show up and say "I do."

Every wedding gets an emergency box. Every couple gets my full attention. And every guest gets a seat, a good meal, and absolutely no rubbery chicken.

Reviewed the services list. I had questions.

- Nate

Services

  • Full planning

    From the first vision board to the last dance. Venue, vendors, budget, timeline, and every detail in between.

  • Partial planning

    You've started; I'll take it from here and make all the pieces fit together.

  • Day-of coordination

    You planned it. I'll run it, so you can actually be there for it.

  • Catering, by Crumb Together Cafe

    My exclusive catering partner: award-winning, wholeheartedly trusted, and never late with the cake.

How it works

  1. The consultationTell me about your dream day. I'll ask a lot of questions. (Fair warning: so will your family.)
  2. Tastings, tours, and the binderWe taste the cake, walk the venue, and build the plan, one color-coded tab at a time.
  3. The big dayI handle the timeline, the vendors, and anything that goes sideways. Most couples never even know.
