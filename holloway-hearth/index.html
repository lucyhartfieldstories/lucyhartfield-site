Order like a regular

Everybody in Holloway has a usual. Here's what the regulars order when they come in from the cold (styled and photographed by Lucy herself).

  • Lila

    London fog

    Her work-day order, in a travel mug, because there's always somewhere to be.

  • Nate

    Pomegranate green tea with vanilla

    Hot, with a shot of vanilla. Lila may or may not make him the syrup.

  • Henry

    Peppermint mocha with raspberry

    Blended. No straw. Don't ask him why, just hand it over.

  • Trish

    Pumpkin spice latte

    Iced, with a story to go with it. (Trish always has a story.)
