The text that started it

This is the message Nate was rereading when he walked straight into a tray of cake. He'd read it three times. He still didn't see the sign.

Kat 💍

Yesterday, 9:41 PM

Hey Nate, are you free at 10am tomorrow? Our cake tasting is at Crumb Together Cafe. I thought you could join to give your opinions. There have been so many options lately, I'm having a hard time making any decisions at all. Please?

Read 9:41 PM. Read again 9:52 PM. Read again 9:58 AM, walking through a bakery lobby.

On my way. Might be a few minutes late.
