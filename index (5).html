Short bio

Lucy Hartfield writes warm, witty romance full of banter, big feelings, and the people who feel like home. Her debut, an enemies-to-lovers romantic comedy set in the world of weddings, is coming soon.

Long bio

Lucy Hartfield writes romance for readers who love sharp banter, soft hearts, and a happily ever after that feels earned. Her stories are about imperfect people finding love, and the communities that hold them up along the way.

Lucy believes books are meant to be shared: passed between friends, argued about in group chats, and read far too late into the night. Her debut novel, an enemies-to-lovers romantic comedy about a detail-obsessed wedding planner and the one guest who wrecks her plans, is coming soon.

By day, Lucy plans events, which taught her that great stories and great celebrations both bring people together. When she's not writing, you'll find her with a seasonal latte, a book, or a tray of seedlings she's determined to keep alive through a Minnesota spring.

The debut in one line

She plans every detail of other people's happily ever afters. He thinks marriage is a business agreement. One wedding, one bed, and one very messy cake tasting.
