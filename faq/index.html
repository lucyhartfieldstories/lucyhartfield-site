Frequently asked questions

Answers about Lucy Hartfield's books, advance copies, and working together.

When is Lucy Hartfield's debut novel coming out?

Lucy is writing her debut now. The title, cover, and release date will be announced to newsletter subscribers first, so joining Letters from Hartfield is the best way to hear the news the moment it's official.

What is the debut about?

It's an instant chemistry, opposites attract romantic comedy set in the world of weddings. Lila, a detail-obsessed wedding planner, collides, literally, with Nate, the bride's practical little brother, at a cake tasting. Now they're stuck together through every fitting, dinner, and crisis until the big day.

What tropes are in the book?

The big three are instant chemistry, opposites attract, and forced proximity. You'll also find a messy meet-cute, a type-A heroine, wedding season chaos, one bed, found family, plenty of banter, and mental health representation written with care.

How can I get an advance copy?

Advance reader copies go to newsletter subscribers first. When the book is ready for early readers, subscribers will get an invitation to apply.

I'm a book blogger, BookToker, or podcaster. Can we work together?

Yes, Lucy would love to hear from you. The media kit has bios, photos, and a book summary you can use, and you can reach her at hello@lucyhartfield.com.

Where can I follow Lucy?

Lucy shares writing updates and book talk on TikTok and Instagram at @lucyhartfieldstories, on Bluesky at @lucyhartfield.com, and on Pinterest, YouTube, Goodreads, and StoryGraph.
